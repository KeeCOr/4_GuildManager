# Privacy Policy — GuildManager

Last updated: 2026-09-05

## Data Collection

GuildManager does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- No account registration required
- No analytics or telemetry
- No network connections initiated by the game
- Save data is stored locally on your device only

## Steam Integration

When launched through Steam, GuildManager uses the Steamworks SDK to:
- Display your Steam username in-game (read-only, never stored externally)
- Unlock Steam achievements (data managed entirely by Valve/Steam)
- Sync save data via Steam Cloud (optional, managed by Steam)

All Steam data is governed by [Valve's Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

## Contact

For questions: contact via the Steam Community Hub for GuildManager.
