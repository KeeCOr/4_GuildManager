# GuildManager — Steam Store Description

## Short Description (30 words)
Build your medieval mercenary guild. Recruit, train, and deploy fighters in tactical turn-based missions. Grow from a ragged band into a legendary war company.

## Full Description

**GuildManager** puts you in charge of a medieval mercenary company.

Recruit fighters from diverse backgrounds — each with unique stats, traits, and skill trees. Send them on contracts, manage their morale and wages, and watch your reputation grow across the region.

**Key Features:**
- **Guild Management** — Hire, fire, train, and equip a roster of mercenaries
- **Tactical Missions** — Turn-based combat on hand-crafted maps
- **Reputation System** — Your guild's fame opens new contract tiers and client relationships
- **Roguelite Progression** — Each campaign run offers different recruits, contracts, and events
- **Advisor System** — AI advisors surface key decisions and strategic options
- **Deep Build Variety** — Combine class specializations and equipment for hundreds of viable squad compositions

---

## About This Game

GuildManager began as a passion project for fans of classic strategy games who want the *management layer* — the decisions that happen *before* the battle — to feel as satisfying as the combat itself.

Every hire is a risk. Every contract is a trade-off. Every loss stings.
